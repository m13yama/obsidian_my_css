Reading view contents now support H1 titles and adjustable width. Configure both under **Settings → Obsidian My CSS → Reading view**.

- **Treat H1 as title** is on by default, including when upgrading from 0.3.0. H1 titles are omitted from the contents and their H2 sections appear at the top level. Turn it off to include H1 headings. H1-only notes have no empty contents panel when this setting is on.
- Remove the generated **Beginning** / **先頭** item so the list contains only note headings.
- Drag the left edge of the contents to resize it, or enter a value under **Table of contents width** and press **Apply** or Enter. The range is **160–600px**, with a default of **224px**.
- Save the width across restarts and share it across open Reading view panes. Smaller panes fit the contents to the available space while retaining the saved preference.
- Double-click the divider or use **Reset** to restore the default width. The divider also supports Left/Right keys, Shift for larger steps, Home/End for the available limits, and Escape to cancel a drag.
